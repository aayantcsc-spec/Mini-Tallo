# Mini Tallo Terms of Use

Version 2026-10.2

This is a plain-language draft. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Have it reviewed before you give Mini Tallo to anyone else.

1. **What it is.** Mini Tallo is software that runs on your computer and lets an AI assistant read the TallyPrime books open on that computer, prepare voucher import files, and, only if you turn posting on, post vouchers you approve one at a time.

2. **Your books, your responsibility.** You are responsible for your books of account and for every entry in them. Check what the assistant tells you against TallyPrime before you rely on it. Mini Tallo's figures are not audited, are not a GST return, and are not financial statements under any accounting standard.

3. **What your AI provider sees.** Whatever the assistant reads through Mini Tallo goes to your AI provider as part of the conversation: company, party and ledger names, amounts, dates, narrations, references, GSTINs and, when asked for, PAN, bank account numbers, IFSC, MSME or Udyam numbers, email, phone and address. The privacy setting reduces some of this and not all of it. Before you use Mini Tallo with a client's data, make sure you are allowed to share that data with your AI provider.

4. **Posting.** A posted voucher cannot be undone by Mini Tallo; you correct it in TallyPrime. Read "Before you turn on posting" in the README first.

5. **Editing from the dashboard.** With "Allow editing" on, the dashboard creates, changes and deletes masters and vouchers in TallyPrime as soon as you save. Mini Tallo cannot undo these. Take a backup first. You are responsible for every change you make this way.

6. **Local connection only.** Mini Tallo connects to TallyPrime on this computer only. Do not forward TallyPrime's port across a network.
